# FINDINGS - Kestrel ops data, verified

Generated by `python profile.py` (read-only). Every number below is recomputed
from the database on each run; nothing is quoted from the data dictionary,
which itself warns that some of its descriptions are wrong.

## F1. Quantities are booked in two units, mixed inside single orders

`order_lines.qty_uom` is CASE on 398,741 lines and EACH on 112,775;
60,288 orders mix both units. Summing `ordered_qty` raw is meaningless, yet gives a
plausible-looking fill rate of 88.07%. Converted properly, fill rate is 85.59% in eaches
and 85.88% in cases. The 2.5pt gap between the naive and the true number is the likely root
of "our numbers never match the customer's numbers" (Rakesh). All quantities in the semantic
layer carry both a `_each` and a `_case` column; the UI defaults to eaches (how customers
penalise) with a cases toggle (how ops commits).

> **Evidence:** fill rate: naive mixed-UoM 88.07% | eaches 85.59% | cases 85.88% (DELIVERED+PARTIAL orders; case_pack_at_order matches products.case_pack on all 511,516 lines)

## F2. KP-2301 root cause: PARTNER_API net order value is inflated by exactly 8.5%

The known-issues log says header value does not reconcile "for one source system,
root cause not established". Established: for every one of the 8,256 PARTNER_API orders,
`order_value_net_inr` = (gross - discount + tax) x 1.085, with the ratio constant to 6 decimal
places. ERP_WEB and SFA_MOBILE reconcile exactly (0 exceptions). Looks like a marketplace
commission or double-applied charge baked into the feed. The semantic layer recomputes net from
components and keeps the feed's value alongside as `net_as_reported`.

> **Evidence:** net/(gross-discount+tax) over all PARTNER_API orders: min 1.084999, max 1.085002 (n=8,256)

## F3. No order line is ever delivered in full - a literal OTIF is identically zero

Across all 511,516 lines the maximum line fill is 99.86%. Even orders with
status DELIVERED are only ~88% filled. Any strict in-full definition yields OTIF = 0 for the
entire 18 months, so the metric would carry no signal. We define in-full as order fill (eaches)
>= 98%, exposed as a single constant (IN_FULL_THRESHOLD in config.py) and flagged on screen.

> **Evidence:** max(delivered_qty/ordered_qty) on non-cancelled lines = 0.9986

## F4. Telematics arrival timestamps have corrupted hour fields; delay_minutes is the trustworthy signal

Recomputing delay from planned vs actual arrival disagrees with the stored
`delay_minutes` on 67,211 of 76,889 deliveries - but the difference is an
exact multiple of 60 minutes on all 76,889 rows (0 exceptions). Minutes always
agree; only the hour is wrong, in both vendors' exports. Stored delay spans a plausible
[-40, 899] minutes; the recomputed one spans [-460, 1318] (deliveries 7.6h
early / 22h late - implausible). OTIF therefore uses `delay_minutes`; arrival timestamps are
used for dates only, never times.

> **Evidence:** rows=76,889; diffs not divisible by 60: 0; stored range [-40,899] vs recomputed [-460,1318]

## F5. Three timestamp formats across source systems; PARTNER_API is UTC, the rest are local

`orders.created_at` arrives as `01/01/2025 18:51` (ERP_WEB), `2025-01-01 16:46:00`
(SFA_MOBILE) and `2025-01-01T03:29:00Z` (PARTNER_API - note the Z). Deliveries add two more
vendor formats (F4). Naive parsing shifts every e-commerce order by 5.5 hours, which moves
late-evening orders to the wrong day. The build normalises everything to IST.

> **Evidence:** ERP_WEB: `01/01/2025 06:13` | PARTNER_API: `2025-01-01T02:49:00Z` | SFA_MOBILE: `2025-01-01 06:05:00`

## F6. Test/migration outlets are live and transacting (KP-2377 confirmed, worse than logged)

3 outlets are obviously synthetic ('ZZ_TEST_OUTLET', 'DO NOT USE - migration dummy', 'TEST KIRANA 01').
All are status ACTIVE with `is_deleted` = 0, so the documented soft-delete filter does NOT
catch them - and they carry 260 orders worth Rs 40.81 crore. Excluded by name
pattern in `dim_outlet.exclude_reason`.

> **Evidence:** id 721 'ZZ_TEST_OUTLET': 85 orders, Rs 13.49cr; id 722 'DO NOT USE - migration dummy': 83 orders, Rs 12.85cr; id 723 'TEST KIRANA 01': 92 orders, Rs 14.47cr

## F7. City names are free text with live duplicates (KP-2288 confirmed)

Bangalore/Bengaluru and Delhi/New Delhi both appear, splitting two metros' outlets
across spellings - any per-city ranking or the competitor price join would silently halve those
cities. Canonicalised via an explicit 2-entry map in the build (19 distinct city values checked
by hand; only these two pairs collide).

> **Evidence:** Bangalore: 16; Bengaluru: 28; Delhi: 27; New Delhi: 11

## F8. Outlet duplicates exist (KP-2211) but only 2 pairs are provable; name-matching would over-merge

128 active name+city combinations collide, but Indian kirana names repeat
legitimately ('Anand Stores' exists in half the cities). Only 2 pairs share a GST
number - those are the same legal entity and are merged. The rest are left alone: for a
control tower, over-merging outlets corrupts every ranking; under-merging only duplicates a
row. Documented as a deliberately conservative call.

> **Evidence:** GST 06ABCDE1525F1Z0: outlets OUT00001,TST00001,TST00002,TST00003; GST 26ABCDE3218F1Z2: outlets OUT00018,OUT90118

## F9. OPEN orders carry delivered quantities but have no delivery notes

10,147 lines on OPEN orders show delivered_qty > 0, yet zero delivery notes exist
for OPEN orders. The quantity has no delivery evidence behind it, so OPEN orders are excluded
from fill-rate and OTIF (they are shown separately as an open-order pipeline count).

> **Evidence:** OPEN lines with delivered_qty>0: 10,147; delivery notes on OPEN orders: 0

## F10. Credit-note quantities carry mixed signs (KP-2402 confirmed); the feed cannot be isolated

900 of 14,000 return quantities are negative. Credit-note numbers share one
prefix and the table has no source-system column, so the offending upstream feed cannot be
identified from the data. Magnitudes are taken as ABS(); the raw sign is preserved in
`qty_sign_as_reported` so the anomaly stays visible rather than being silently 'fixed'.

> **Evidence:** negative: 900; non-negative: 13,100

## F11. Nothing blocks discontinued SKUs at order entry

14,370 order lines (Rs 39.47 crore) were booked after the SKU's discontinuation
date - across effectively every outlet. This is a process gap, not a data error: order capture
does not validate against product status. Surfaced as a dashboard exception view (brief Q8).

> **Evidence:** lines after discontinuation: 14,370, value Rs 39.47cr

## F12. Exclusions must be metric-aware: closed/deleted outlets hold Rs ~267 crore of history

Dropping non-active outlets globally would understate every historical total and
distort returns-as-%-of-dispatch. The layer therefore keeps all facts and stamps
`dim_outlet.exclude_reason`; *rankings and current-state views* filter on it, *historical
totals* do not.

> **Evidence:** ACTIVE: 72,551 orders, Rs 1772.1cr; CLOSED: 6,308 orders, Rs 151.4cr; DELETED: 4,812 orders, Rs 115.8cr

## F13. The data dictionary is wrong in both directions - every claim needed verification

Verified against the data: header gross DOES tie to line sums exactly (all 83,671
orders, all three systems) - the real KP-2301 defect is in net value (F2). `short_reason_code`
is 'populated where short' - trivially, because every line is short (F3). `approval_date` never
populated: confirmed (0 of 14,000). The build trusts only what was verified.

> **Evidence:** gross-vs-lines exceptions: 0; approval_date populated: 0/14,000

## F15. Credit notes are dated past the stated period end

393 credit notes carry return dates in July 2026, after the pack's stated
30 June 2026 cut-off - returns lag the orders they credit. A calendar that stops at period end
silently drops them (it did, in an early build). The analytics calendar runs to 2026-07-29 and
June-quarter returns metrics note that late credits may still be arriving ('the quarter is not
closed for returns').

> **Evidence:** returns dated after 2026-06-30: 393; latest return_date: 2026-07-29

## F14. What is clean (and is relied on)

Order numbers unique (0 dupes), no orphan lines (0), deliveries map
1:1 to DELIVERED/PARTIAL orders (0 multi-delivery orders), `case_pack_at_order` agrees
with the product master on every line, and `product_price_history` covers all 341 SKUs with
gapless validity windows - so price-as-at-order-date is computed exactly, never approximated
from today's price.

> **Evidence:** dupe order numbers: 0; orphan lines: 0; orders with >1 delivery: 0
